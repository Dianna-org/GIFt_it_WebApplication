# Instagram plan

I can't create the account for you (account creation has to be done by you), but everything else is ready to copy.

## Set-up (10 minutes)
1. Create a new Instagram account → switch to a **Creator** or **Business** (professional) account in Settings (free, gives insights).
2. Name ideas (check availability): `cardstudio.app`, `cozycardstudio`, `makeacard.studio`, `pocketcardstudio`.
3. **Bio** (≤150 chars):
   > 🎂 Make a birthday card on a cosy digital craft desk
   > ✂️ tape · stickers · glitter · markers
   > 👇 try it free
4. Profile picture: the folded card on the yellow desk (crop a screenshot of the app).
5. Link: your deployed site (or a free Linktree-style page until it's deployed).
6. Highlights: "How it works", "Cards by you", "Updates".

## Content pillars
1. **Making-of time-lapses** (60% of posts) – a card from blank to done, with the tape being cut and stickers peeling.
2. **Satisfying close-ups** – glitter pen sparkle, tape pull-and-cut, marker vs highlighter layers.
3. **Show the exports** – the GIF layouts (candles flickering, confetti rain), ready to send.
4. **Behind the scenes** – "how I built it" for a student/maker audience (React, ASP.NET, MongoDB).
5. **Community** – feature cards users made (ask permission first).

## Caption template
> Hook (one line). What it is in one sentence. "Free, runs in your browser – link in bio." Question to invite comments.

## Safety
The app is for 10+ but accounts for 14+. Don't target ads at under-13s, don't ask kids for personal info in comments/DMs, and never show a user's card without their permission.
